About Me

Industrial Engineer · Air Transport & Logistics · Analytics · Process Improvement

Hello, I'm Mustafa

I am an Industrial and Manufacturing Engineer currently completing an MSc in Air Transport and Logistics at TU Dresden.

My background combines industrial engineering, logistics, transport planning, data analytics, simulation, optimisation and digital process improvement.

Professional Experience

Alongside my Master's studies, I work at Infineon Technologies in Quality Management within the international oneSAP transformation environment.

My work includes developing and maintaining structured knowledge and process documentation, supporting international SAP integration testing, and contributing to digitalisation projects in quality management.

I have co-developed a process-mining solution for Supplier Quality Management processes and contributed to an AI-assisted text-mining solution for extracting and structuring information from calibration certificates.

Transport & Logistics

Through my MSc in Air Transport and Logistics, I am expanding my industrial-engineering background into transport systems, aviation, operations research and logistics planning.

My academic projects include transport-network optimisation, line-network planning, GIS-based logistics modelling and operations-research applications.

Simulation & Optimisation

Simulation has been an important part of my engineering background since my Bachelor's studies.

I have used discrete-event simulation to analyse industrial logistics, warehouse operations, material-handling systems, service processes and transport-related problems.

Rather than treating simulation as an isolated technical skill, I use it as one of several analytical tools for understanding complex operational systems and evaluating alternative decisions.
